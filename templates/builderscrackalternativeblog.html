What is Builderscrack?

Builderscrack has been around since 2008 and was one of the first online platforms in New Zealand to connect homeowners with tradies. The concept was solid: post a job, get quotes, choose a tradie. For its time, it was genuinely useful.

But the platform has aged and has been sold to Australian owners. And as newer, simpler alternatives have emerged, the friction points that Kiwi homeowners and tradespeople quietly put up with have become harder to ignore.

The Problems With Builderscrack (That Nobody Talks About)

If you've used Builderscrack - or tried to - you've probably run into at least one of these:

  • The bidding system is stressful. You post a job, and then wait. Multiple tradies quote. For a straightforward job, it's overkill.
  • Fees on both sides. Tradies pay to access leads. That cost gets baked into their quotes - which means you end up paying it anyway.
  • No guarantee of contact. A tradie might quote and then go quiet. You're back to square one, having wasted days.
  • It can feel impersonal. The platform processes jobs like tickets. Home renovations are personal - you want to deal with a real person.
  • High upfront subscription costs. Builderscrack requires tradies to pay monthly fees before securing any work, meaning costs are incurred even when no jobs are generated.

None of this is unique to Builderscrack - it's a pattern across older lead-generation platforms. The model made sense in 2010. In 2026, there's a better way.
